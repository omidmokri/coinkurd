Flags: flag-icons 7.6.0 by Panayiotis Lipiridis (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence (LICENSE in this folder), 1x1 variant.
One flag per currency the /market/ page can show prices in (the issuing country; the EU for the euro; Iran for the toman).
Flags heavier than 12 kB in the original (coats of arms: rs, bo, mx, bz, do, gt, tm, fk, ky, fj, bm, bn, ir, py, ni, ht)
are the same flag drawn at 96 x 96 and kept as a WebP inside the SVG, so a 20-pixel flag never costs 180 kB.
